Method

How the numbers are checked

Most AI supply chain coverage is either a one-off explainer or a paywalled analyst deck. This is an open, maintained reference: every number carries a dated source, a confidence rating, and the date a person last checked it.

The map

The chain is split into nine tiers, from the machines that make chips (T1) to the services that deliver AI answers (T9). Each box is a component: a product category such as "HBM memory", not a company. Lines show what feeds what. Components still in the research queue use planned links, which are replaced by sourced ones as each card is written.

The research pipeline

  1. Draft. A fast model searches the web and fills in the card. Every number must cite a URL from the search results; it may leave a value blank but may not invent one.
  2. Verify. A stronger model from a different family reads every cited source and checks: does the source say this, is it current, is the confidence fair?
  3. Approve. A person reviews the card and the verification report, fixes what needs fixing, and signs it off. Nothing is marked approved without that step.
  4. Refresh. Every month the pipeline re-runs on approved cards and proposes changes for review.

Card status

  • Planned Research has not started.
  • Draft Written by the pipeline, not yet checked.
  • Verified Checked against its sources by a second model.
  • Approved Reviewed and signed off by a person.
